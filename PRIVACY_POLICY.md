# Privacy Policy for NoteSticky

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 2, 2026  

**NoteSticky** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how NoteSticky handles user data.

---

### 1. No Data Collection or Transmission
NoteSticky is designed with a strict offline-first, privacy-first philosophy:
- **We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**
- **We do NOT track your browsing history, analytics, or user behavior.**
- **We do NOT operate any remote servers, databases, or third-party tracking services.**
- **We do NOT sell, lease, or monetize your data under any circumstances.**

---

### 2. Local Storage and Data Usage
All information created while using NoteSticky—including note contents, checklist items, background colors, note coordinates, and domain URLs—is stored **exclusively on your local computer** within Chrome's isolated storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

- Your notes remain entirely under your control on your machine.
- Exporting or importing notes is performed locally through JSON files on your device.
- Uninstalling the extension or clearing extension data will remove all stored notes from your local machine.

---

### 3. Permissions Explanation
NoteSticky requests the following browser permissions solely to provide its core functionality:
- **`storage`**: Used to save your sticky notes, checklists, and preferences locally in your browser.
- **`activeTab`**: Used to anchor and position a new sticky note on the currently active tab when requested.
- **`contextMenus`**: Used to provide a convenient right-click context menu item ("Add Sticky Note") to create notes on the current page.
- **`<all_urls>`**: Required to allow you to place, view, and interact with your sticky notes on any webpage you visit.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
NoteSticky does not include any third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics), advertising networks, or external SDKs.

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions, revisions will be posted directly to this repository.
