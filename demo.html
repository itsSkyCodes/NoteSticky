🚀 How to Test Sticky Notes on this Page

  1. Look at the bottom right corner of your screen — you should see the Floating Quick Toolbar (➕ New Note).
  2. Click "➕ New Note" to create a sticky note anywhere on this page.
  3. Try dragging the note by its top header bar. Notice how smooth and responsive it feels!
  4. Click 🎨 to change its color to Mint, Coral, Lavender, Ocean Sky, or Dark Slate.
  5. Click ☑️ to switch to interactive checklist/todo mode. Add some tasks and check them off!
  6. Click 👁️ on any sticky note header to completely hide that specific sticky note popup. Click the Hide/Show (👁️ / 🙈) button in the Floating Quick Toolbar to hide or show all open sticky note popups simultaneously! When popups overlap, clicking any sticky note underneath immediately brings it to the top and makes it active!
  7. Press Alt+Shift+N (or MacCtrl+Shift+N) to spawn a new note via keyboard shortcut.
  8. Select any text on this page, right-click, and choose "Create Sticky Note from..." from the context menu.
  9. Open the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar to view the full Popup Dashboard, search your notes, or jump straight to them!
